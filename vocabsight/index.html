Support

Learning stays on your device.

VocabSight bundles its beginner word catalog, visual anchors, reviewed examples, and pronunciation resources with the app. Core learning requires no account or internet connection.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings and choose Restore Purchase while signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Reset learning progress

Settings → Reset Learning Progress removes local learning history and review schedules while keeping settings and purchases.

Report a content issue

Use Settings → Content & Privacy → Content Correction. You can review the email before sending it.

Need help? Include the VocabSight version, iOS or iPadOS version, the word or screen involved, and what you expected. Never send Apple Account credentials. Email legion.xiong@gmail.com.
